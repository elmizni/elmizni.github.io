The microphone is blocked for this page. On iPhone, tap the aA button in Safari's address bar, choose Website Settings, and set Microphone to Allow. In Chrome, tap the icon to the left of the address and allow the microphone. Then tap Record.
Keep it as an app: tap Safari's Share button, then Add to Home Screen. A Recorder icon will appear with your other apps.
