Solar widget for your iPhone

Your solar system on the home screen and lock screen, in three sizes and several layouts. It uses the free Scriptable app; your VRM key stays in the iPhone's keychain.

Preview with sample numbers. The real widget shows yours.

1

Install Scriptable

It's free, from the App Store.

Open the App Store
2

Copy the widget

Copied. Now go to step 3.
3

Paste it into Scriptable

  1. Open Scriptable and tap + at the top right.
  2. Tap the empty page, then tap Paste.
  3. Tap the title Untitled Script at the top and rename it Solar.
  4. Tap Done.
4

Connect it once

  1. Come back to this page and tap Copy my VRM key.
  2. Open Scriptable and tap the ▶ on the Solar card.
  3. Tap Use copied key. A menu then lets you preview every layout. Tap Done when finished.
5

Add it to your home screen

  1. Press and hold an empty spot on the home screen, then tap Edit at the top left and Add Widget.
  2. Find Scriptable, swipe to the size you want, and tap Add Widget.
  3. Press and hold the new widget and tap Edit Widget.
  4. Set Script to Solar. In Parameter, type one of the layout names below, or leave it empty for the default.

default		Solar, home, battery and grid right now

6

Lock screen (optional)

  1. Press and hold the lock screen, tap Customize, then Lock Screen.
  2. Tap the widget area under the clock, choose Scriptable, and pick the circle (battery), rectangle (summary) or the line above the clock.
  3. Tap the widget you added and set Script to Solar.

Tapping the widget opens your Solar Home page. iPhone refreshes widgets on its own schedule, usually every 10–20 minutes, and the numbers are the ones Victron last received from your system; the time in the corner tells you how recent they are.
